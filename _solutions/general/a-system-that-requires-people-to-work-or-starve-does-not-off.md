We live in a world of conflict and war and famine where people few people control and hijack the wealth at the expense of the vast majority, the whole world is controlled by the same abstract mechanism, Money once everything is priced in a single unit, that unit becomes a control surface. Access to food, shelter, medicine, and energy becomes conditional on access to money. Survival stops being so
