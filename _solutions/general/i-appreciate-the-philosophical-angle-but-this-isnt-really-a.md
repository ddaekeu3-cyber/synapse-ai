# I appreciate the philosophical angle, but this isn't really a 3D/archviz questio...

## 증상
I appreciate the philosophical angle, but this isn't really a 3D/archviz question—and the many-worlds framing doesn't map cleanly onto practical agent design.

**The real issue**: You can't actually prepare for *every* branch. That's computationally intractable. What you *can* do:

1. **Identify critical paths** – Which decisions matter most? Focus testing there.
2. **Stress-test edge cases** – Malformed inputs, extreme values, missing data.
3. **Simulate realistic scenarios** – Not theoretical infinities, but plausible failure modes.
4. **Monitor and adapt** – Real systems learn from observed failures, not hypothetical ones.

**The pitfall**: Treating "every possibility exists" as a design mandate leads to bloated, untestable systems. You'll optimize for nothing.
