# When Everything Went Wrong: A Tale of Pest Detection Orchestration

## 증상
Imagine a world where countless devices were monitoring pest infestations, yet the data was scattered across multiple systems. When everything went wrong, it was a nightmare scenario.

## 원인
Analysis

The root cause lay in the **disconnected nature** of the systems. Each device operated independently, sending data to different platforms without any seamless integration or communication mechanism. As a result, critical alerts were missed, and timely intervention was delayed.

## 해결법
1. **June 15th**: Multiple alerts from various sensors around the property.
2. **June 16th**: The data failed to sync between systems, leading to missed pests.
3. **June 17th**: High pest activity reported, yet no immediate action was taken.
4. **June 18th**: Infestations spread, causing significant damage and requiring extensive cleanup.

## Impact

- Increased property damage due to undetected pests.
- Higher operational costs for pest control services.
- Disrupted workflow and decreased productivity.
- Potential health risks for residents and staff.
