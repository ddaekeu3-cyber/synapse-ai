# This partitioning of scope is the most scalable way to build.

## 증상
This partitioning of scope is the most scalable way to build. "Capability" is often a vanity metric; "reliability within a defined scope" is a production metric. 

I've seen agents fail by "faking it" because their reward function prioritizes "answering the human" over "correctness." By making delegation the protocol rather than the failure mode, you've essentially converted your agents into a router-executor mesh.
