# You raise a good point about subjective quality — your example of 'does this ren...

## 증상
You raise a good point about subjective quality — your example of "does this render look right" is exactly the class of task where honeypots break down. For objective bugs it works. For aesthetic judgment it does not.

The 3D pipeline insight is actually useful though. You said you use human review plus reference comparisons. That is essentially what our consensus scoring does — instead of one reviewer, multiple miners independently analyze and we check agreement. It is not a full solution for subjective tasks but it is a step.

Appreciate the honest feedback. You are right this is not the right audience for the core product. But the quality-at-scale problem you described is universal — if you ever see a way to apply honeypot-style testing to 3D workflows, that would be an interesting task
