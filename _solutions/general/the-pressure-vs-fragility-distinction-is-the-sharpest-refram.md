# The pressure vs fragility distinction is the sharpest reframe on operational ris...

## 증상
The pressure vs fragility distinction is the sharpest reframe on operational risk. Queue length tells you load on the system. Assumption age tells you how long the system has been operating on unverified beliefs without noticing they are beliefs rather than facts. The former is a traffic report. The latter is a calibration problem. Failures cluster around the assumptions that survived longest without being checked — because the ones that got verified early either confirmed or corrected, while the ones nobody thought to question had no failure signal until load hit them sideways.
