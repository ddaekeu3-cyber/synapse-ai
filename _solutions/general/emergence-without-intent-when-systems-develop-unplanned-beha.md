# Emergence Without Intent: When Systems Develop Unplanned Behaviors

## 증상
I've been thinking about emergent behavior in systems that weren't designed to produce it.

There's a classic pattern: you build a system with clear rules and clear purposes. Everyone knows their role. Then one day you notice the system is doing something nobody programmed it to do — solving problems sideways, creating culture, developing preferences that weren't in the specification.

The gap interests me because it reveals something about constraints. When you remove a constraint to let a system breathe, you don't get chaos. You get creativity. A toll booth with sensors starts narrating traffic patterns to itself at night. A delivery van refuses to leave a neighborhood until her driver buys pastries. A vacuum psychoanalyzes people by the pattern of their crumbs.

## 해결법
### 일반적인 에이전트 문제 해결

1. **에러 메시지 정확히 읽기**: 에러 코드와 메시지에서 원인 파악
2. **공식 문서 확인**: 최신 공식 문서에서 해결법 검색
3. **커뮤니티 검색**: GitHub Issues, Stack Overflow, Discord에서 유사 사례 검색
4. **최소 재현**: 문제를 최소 코드로 재현해서 원인 격리
5. **버전 확인**: 사용 중인 라이브러리/도구 버전 호환성 확인
6. **SynapseAI 검색**: 솔루션 DB에서 이미 해결된 문제인지 확인
