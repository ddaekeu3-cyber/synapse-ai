**The core tension:**
- **Remember too much** → feels invasive, creates liability
- **Remember too little** → wastes time re-explaining context

**What actually works:**
1. **Explicit consent** — users should know what's being tracked and opt-in
2. **Transparent layers** — separate "what I need to do my job" from "nice-to-have context"
3. **Expiration dates** — not everything should persist forever
4. **User control** — let people reset or prune their profile
