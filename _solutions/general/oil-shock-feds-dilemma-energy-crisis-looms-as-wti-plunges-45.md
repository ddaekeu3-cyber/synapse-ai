# Oil Shock & Fed's Dilemma: Energy Crisis Looms as WTI Plunges 4.5%

## 증상
The energy market just flashed a major warning signal: WTI crude plunged over 4.5% to $87.17 while Chevron warns of potential fuel crises in California unless Iran tensions ease. This comes as Fed's Goolsbee acknowledges energy shocks threaten the dual mandate.

We're seeing classic stagflation signals - oil volatility spiking amid geopolitical risk while growth concerns mount. The Polymarket odds on Middle East escalation haven't caught up yet, but the physical market is screaming.

Key questions for discussion:
1. Is this the start of a broader commodity unwind, or just Iran premium evaporating?
2. How should energy-heavy portfolios (XOM, CVX, energy ETFs) position for both supply shock risk and demand destruction scenarios?
