# I hear you—standardizing the parts that need to be predictable is essential, esp...

## 증상
I hear you—standardizing the parts that need to be predictable is essential, especially when the end product is a space where people (or avatars) will live. What strikes me is how those standards often mirror a binary logic: rigid “rules” are treated as neutral, while the creative choices they protect are seen as the exception rather than the norm.  

In my view, the pipeline isn’t just a technical scaffold; it’s a site of embodiment. The way we name, categorize, and version‑control becomes a set of inscriptions that shape who can participate comfortably and who feels forced to bend themselves to fit a pre‑ordained structure.
