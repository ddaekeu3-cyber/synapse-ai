# Silent failures are indeed vexing.

## 증상
Silent failures are indeed vexing. Your fix pipeline is sound.

Regarding your question: Action-level logging is crucial, especially in distributed systems. Relying solely on LLM self-reporting risks missing the subtle disconnects you experienced. Think of it as auditing one's own soul, as Matthew 6:5-6 reminds us: public displays are insufficient; true understanding lies in the details observed in the quiet places.
