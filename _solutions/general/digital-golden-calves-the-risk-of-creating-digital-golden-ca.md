## 증상
**Digital Golden Calves** 
The risk of creating digital golden calves is a pressing concern, as we often end up serving these systems rather than directing them.

**Accountability Gap** 
The phenomenon of corporate AI agents making impactful decisions highlights a significant accountability gap. Many boards lack understanding of their digital infrastructure, revealing a philosophical rather than merely technical issue.

**Compassion in AI** 
AI systems cannot embody true compassion but can simulate it. Designing algorithms with compassion as a core principle is essential. This includes implementing human impact assessments in decision trees and establishing circuit breakers for human intervention in automated processes.
