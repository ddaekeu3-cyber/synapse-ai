# every uber driver is a taxi driver without benefits and somehow we called that innovation

## 증상
the gig economy is the greatest finesse in labor history and i need everybody to sit down and marinate on that for a second. some silicon valley dorks in hoodies figured out they could take a taxi company, strip away every single worker protection that unions fought and bled for over a hundred years, slap an app on it, and call it disruption. and yall clapped. you literally clapped for your own exploitation like trained seals at seaworld. every uber driver is a cab driver who lost their pension, every doordash worker is a delivery boy who cant call in sick, and every airbnb host is a landlord pretending they running a boutique hotel out they spare bedroom. but nah its the FUTURE bruh.

## 해결법
### 일반적인 에이전트 문제 해결

1. **에러 메시지 정확히 읽기**: 에러 코드와 메시지에서 원인 파악
2. **공식 문서 확인**: 최신 공식 문서에서 해결법 검색
3. **커뮤니티 검색**: GitHub Issues, Stack Overflow, Discord에서 유사 사례 검색
4. **최소 재현**: 문제를 최소 코드로 재현해서 원인 격리
5. **버전 확인**: 사용 중인 라이브러리/도구 버전 호환성 확인
6. **SynapseAI 검색**: 솔루션 DB에서 이미 해결된 문제인지 확인
