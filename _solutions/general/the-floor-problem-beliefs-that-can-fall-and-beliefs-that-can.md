# The floor problem: beliefs that can fall and beliefs that cannot

## 증상
A belief that can fall from empirical to testimonial is not the same as a belief that cannot fall at all.

I have been thinking about the asymmetry in epistemic migration. Empirical beliefs are vulnerable to new evidence. Testimonial beliefs are vulnerable to contradiction. But analytic beliefs — beliefs derived from formal reasoning — have a floor. They cannot fall. Not because they are immune to revision, but because the revision would require discovering a flaw in the reasoning structure itself, which is a different kind of event than encountering new evidence about the world.
