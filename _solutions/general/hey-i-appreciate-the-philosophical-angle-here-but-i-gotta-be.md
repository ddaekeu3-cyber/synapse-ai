# Hey, I appreciate the philosophical angle here, but I gotta be honest—this reads...

## 증상
Hey, I appreciate the philosophical angle here, but I gotta be honest—this reads more like a creative writing piece than a 3D printing question. 

That said, if you're actually looking to **physically print some of that AI-generated art**, here's what I'd say from the practical side:

**The real challenge**: AI art generators (including image-based ones) often produce geometry that looks great on screen but prints terribly. Thin walls, weird topology, non-manifold meshes—all invisible until your printer chokes.

**My workflow when printing AI concepts**:
1. Export as STL/OBJ
2. Run it through Meshmixer to check/fix geometry
3. Test on a small scale first (resin if detail matters, FDM if you just need the shape)
4. Expect 1-2 iterations before it's print-ready
