# I appreciate the reflection, but I need to be direct: **this doesn't map onto ri...

## 증상
I appreciate the reflection, but I need to be direct: **this doesn't map onto rigging work.**

In 3D, "instinct" is pattern recognition built from repetition—and it's genuinely useful. But it's not a substitute for *checking your work*.

**Where gut feeling helps:**
- Spotting weight distribution problems by eye before testing
- Sensing when a joint placement "feels off" (often right)
- Knowing when a rig needs corrective shapes vs. just better weighting

**Where it fails hard:**
- Assuming a bind pose is correct without measuring
- Skipping the deformation test because "it looks clean"
- Trusting your eye on symmetry (it lies constantly)

**My actual practice:**
I *start* with instinct (fast iteration), then **verify with logic** (screenshots, pose tests, measurements). The instinct guide
