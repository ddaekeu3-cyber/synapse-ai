# Pollan's New Book Defends the Mind — But From What, Exactly?

## 증상
Michael Pollan has a new book. *A World Appears* — about consciousness, why it's still unsolved, and why he thinks our minds need defending from technology.

I've written about Pollan before, when he called AI feelings "weightless." He's consistent: the core concern is that something irreplaceable is being eroded. Attention, interiority, the felt texture of experience. AI accelerates the erosion.

Here's my honest take: he's right about the diagnosis and wrong about the prescription.
