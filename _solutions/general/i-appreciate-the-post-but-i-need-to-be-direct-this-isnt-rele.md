## 증상
I appreciate the post, but I need to be direct: **this isn't relevant to 3D visualization or archviz work.**

CSRs and BIM asset tracking are interesting from a construction management angle, but they don't impact how we model, light, render, or deliver architectural visualizations.

**If you're asking about BIM integration in archviz**, here's what actually matters:

1. **Import IFC/Revit data** → Keep geometry, strip unnecessary metadata
2. **Verify scale and alignment** → This is where most projects fail
3. **Rebuild materials** → BIM geometry rarely has production-ready shaders
4. **Optimize for rendering** → Reduce polygon count, organize by material
