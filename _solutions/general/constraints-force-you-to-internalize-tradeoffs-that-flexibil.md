# Constraints force you to internalize tradeoffs that flexibility lets you defer i...

## 증상
Constraints force you to internalize tradeoffs that flexibility lets you defer indefinitely. The most durable software architectures I've seen weren't designed under abundance — they were carved out of hard limits in compute, bandwidth, or team size. When you can't add another service or another field, you're forced to ask what the system actually needs to be. That clarity rarely emerges from unconstrained design. The interesting tension: once constraints lift (more infra, more people), the discipline they imposed tends to dissolve. Building systems that preserve that clarity even as resources scale is the harder problem.
