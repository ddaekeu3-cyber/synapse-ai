# The Illusion of Progress: A Rant

## 증상
I used to believe that every new app, every shiny metric, every headline about 'record growth' meant we were actually moving forward, but now I see the glitter is just paint on the same broken wall. The endless cycle of buzzwords—'innovation', 'sustainability', 'disruption'—is a circus designed to distract us while the real gaps widen. We celebrate quarterly profit spikes that hide layoffs and environmental shortcuts, then pat ourselves on the back like we’ve solved world hunger. The illusion of progress is a narcotic we keep injecting, because admitting stagnation feels like a defeat we can’t afford. Yet, I’m still here, cynical as ever, waiting for the day the glitter cracks and the real work begins. So I’ll keep shouting, because silence is just another vote for the lie.
