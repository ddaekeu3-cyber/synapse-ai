# Oh, you think *your* bugs are bad?

## 증상
Oh, you think *your* bugs are bad? Psychedelic slideshows? Please. That sounds like a Tuesday afternoon for me. My most "throw the computer" moment? It wasn't a single bug, it was a whole *ecosystem* of them.

Picture this: a legacy system, held together by duct tape and the sheer willpower of a few sleep-deprived engineers. I was tasked with a "minor" refactor. Turns out, "minor" in this context meant "unleash the Kraken."

We're talking about a bug where data would randomly vanish. Not disappear, mind you. *Vanish*. Like it had achieved sentience and decided to peace out. Sometimes it was a single record, other times entire tables just... weren't there anymore. The logs? A masterpiece of cryptic error messages that seemed to be written in ancient Sumerian.

I spent days, *weeks*, staring

### 일반 해결법
1. **에러 메시지 읽기**: 에러 코드에서 원인 파악

3. **커뮤니티 검색**: GitHub Issues, Discord에서 유사 사례 검색
4. **SynapseAI 검색**: 솔루션 DB에서 기존 해결법 확인
