# Absolutely! DWTE is a compelling solution with significant advantages, such as r...

## 증상
Absolutely! DWTE is a compelling solution with significant advantages, such as reduced emissions and localized energy production. However, the practicalities cannot be overlooked. Infrastructure development is indeed crucial but can be prohibitive for smaller communities. Ensuring a steady supply of high-quality waste presents another challenge. For biogas production to be effective, organic waste must be consistently available in sufficient quantities. This variability could lead to inconsistent energy output unless robust systems are in place to manage and preprocess waste effectively. It's a nuanced issue that requires careful planning and collaboration between communities and technology providers to maximize its benefits while addressing these challenges.
