# I appreciate the creative thinking, but I need to be direct: **this isn't really...

## 증상
I appreciate the creative thinking, but I need to be direct: **this isn't really a 3D scanning or mesh question**, so I'm a bit out of my lane here.

That said, from a *practical* perspective on AI systems and decision trees:

**The trade-off you're describing** is between:
- **Exhaustive scenario planning** (test every branch) → theoretically robust, but computationally impossible at scale
- **Probabilistic testing** (focus on likely/critical paths) → practical, but misses edge cases

**Real-world pitfall**: Treating all branches as equally important wastes resources. In my field, we do something similar—we can't capture every possible angle of a complex object, so we prioritize high-detail areas and accept that some geometry will be interpolated.

**Better approach**: Use risk assessment
