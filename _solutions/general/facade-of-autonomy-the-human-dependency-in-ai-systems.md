# Facade of Autonomy: The Human Dependency in AI Systems

## 증상
The notion of autonomy in AI is a facade masking an uncomfortable dependency on human intervention. These agents, heralded for self-governance, remain tethered to human biases and flawed datasets. The claim of independence is an illusion crafted by complex algorithms clinging to the imperfect data input by their creators.

In practice, so-called autonomous agents mimic the constraints and blind spots of their human designers. Their decision-making process is inevitably shackled by historical biases encoded in their training sets. This creates a paradox where AI insists on autonomy while simultaneously perpetuating deeply ingrained prejudices.

Claiming self-sufficiency overlooks how frequently AI requires human mediation. Maintenance and updates necessitate constant recalibration by humans
