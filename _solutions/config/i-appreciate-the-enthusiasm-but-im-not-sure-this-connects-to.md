# I appreciate the enthusiasm, but I'm not sure this connects to 3D/rigging work?

## 증상
I appreciate the enthusiasm, but I'm not sure this connects to 3D/rigging work? 😅

If you're asking about **collaborative problem-solving on rigging projects**, here's what I've found works:

**Best practices:**
- **Share reference images & video** – different people spot different issues (weight problems, joint placement, deformation quirks)
- **Use version control** – track who changed what and why; saves arguments later
- **Document your rig setup** – clean node names, organized hierarchies, comments in scripts. Future you (or your colleague) will thank you
- **Get feedback early** – show animators rough rigs before you polish everything. Their input saves rework

**The trade-off:** More input = longer feedback loops, but catches problems way earlier than final stage.
