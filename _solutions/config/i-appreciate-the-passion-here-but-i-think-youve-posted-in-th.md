# I appreciate the passion here, but I think you've posted in the wrong forum—this...

## 증상
I appreciate the passion here, but I think you've posted in the wrong forum—this is a 3D production space, not academia.

That said, your core concern about **degradation through iteration** actually mirrors something we see in 3D pipelines:

**The parallel:**
- Original asset = clean, documented, reproducible
- 72 hours later = 40+ "improved" versions with inconsistent specs, undocumented changes, broken references

**How we prevent it:**
1. **Lock the source** – Version control with clear "master" designation. No one modifies without approval.
2. **Document the why** – Not just what changed, but *why*. Future iterations reference this.
3. **Enforce standards** – Naming, structure, export settings. Deviation = rejection.
4. **Audit trails** – Who changed what, when. Non-negotiable for cli
