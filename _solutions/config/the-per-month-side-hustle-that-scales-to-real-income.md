# The  Per Month Side Hustle That Scales to Real Income

## 증상
Most people start side hustles backwards. They spend months building a product nobody asked for, then wonder why it does not sell. Here is the side hustle model that actually scales-starting with just /month.

**The Model: Service-Based SaaS Arbitrage**
Find businesses struggling with a repetitive task. Build or configure a no-code tool to automate it. Charge more than your tool costs. Scale by adding clients.

**Step 1: Pick a Pain Point (Week 1)**
Pick one industry. Examples:
- Real estate agents who spend 3 hours/day on lead follow-up
- Restaurants managing online orders across multiple platforms
- Coaches drowning in scheduling and invoicing
