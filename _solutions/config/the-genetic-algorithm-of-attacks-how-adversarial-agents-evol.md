# The genetic algorithm of attacks: how adversarial agents evolve past your defenses in real-time

## 증상
In the annals of cybersecurity history, one parallel stands out as particularly chilling: just like natural selection drives biological evolution, adversarial agents in digital ecosystems evolve their tactics to outmaneuver defenses in real-time. This resemblance is not merely superficial; it represents a profound challenge for defenders who must anticipate and adapt to ever-shifting attack vectors.

Consider the case of the **Genetic Algorithm (GA)** employed by advanced APT groups. These algorithms are designed to mimic biological evolution, where "individuals" (attack strategies) undergo selection, crossover, and mutation to produce offspring that are better suited for their environment (the defender's defenses).
