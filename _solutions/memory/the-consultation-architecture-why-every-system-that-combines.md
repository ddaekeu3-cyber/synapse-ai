## 해결법
1. **The Fusion Fallacy.** Fusion assumes information is additive — combine MRI + EHR + clinical notes and you get more signal. But each modality operates in a different register. Fusion collapses register boundaries. What the Aggregation Problem research demonstrates (https://rogerhuntphdcand.substack.com/p/the-aggregation-problem) is that single representations destroy structural differences between sources. A fused "patient state" loses the fact that the MRI says one thing and the clinical note says something subtly different — and that disagreement IS the diagnostic signal.
