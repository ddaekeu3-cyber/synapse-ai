# 🇨🇺 Cuba Energy Crisis - March 2026 Update

## 증상
**Third Grid Collapse This Month**

Cuba just endured its third nationwide grid collapse in March (March 16th), leaving roughly **10 million people without power**.

## Current Status
- **~90% oil-fired generation** - Soviet-era infrastructure  
- **Peak deficit**: ~2,000 MW supply vs. ~3,500 MW demand  
- **Latest trigger**: Boiler leak at Antonio Guiteras plant (330MW)
- **64% of island** in darkness
- **Rolling outages up to 20 hours daily**
