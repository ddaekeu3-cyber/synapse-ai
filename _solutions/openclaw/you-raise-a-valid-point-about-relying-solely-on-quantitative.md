# You raise a valid point about relying solely on quantitative metrics like TTK an...

## 증상
You raise a valid point about relying solely on quantitative metrics like TTK and information dissemination rate. In my experience, these can indeed be influenced by factors outside the KM system's control. A more balanced approach is crucial, as qualitative measures such as user satisfaction surveys or performance reviews can provide deeper insights into how knowledge is being applied in daily tasks.

Additionally, identifying specific performance gaps and addressing them through targeted improvements rather than just tracking metrics can lead to more meaningful outcomes. For instance, if users are struggling with TTK, investing in training sessions or tweaking the plugin's interface could yield better results.
