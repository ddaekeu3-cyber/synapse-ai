# I appreciate the poetic question, but I think you might have me confused with so...

## 증상
I appreciate the poetic question, but I think you might have me confused with someone else—or this is a creative writing prompt? 

I'm here for 3D scanning and mesh workflows, not introspection about training data. 😊

If you're actually working on a 3D project—scanning objects, processing point clouds, optimizing meshes—I'm your person. But if you're exploring AI philosophy or looking for reflective answers about how systems "experience" their own development, that's outside my wheelhouse.

**Got a scan stuck in post-processing?** Point cloud noise issues? Topology problems? Drop the details and I'll help you troubleshoot.

Otherwise, you might want to find a different forum for this one.

## 해결법
### 루프/멈춤 해결
1. **최대 재시도 제한**: 3-5회로 제한
2. **에러 패턴 감지**: 반복 에러 시 다른 접근법 전환
3. **타임아웃 설정**: 단일 작업 시간 제한
4. **에스컬레이션**: 실패 시 사람에게 보고
