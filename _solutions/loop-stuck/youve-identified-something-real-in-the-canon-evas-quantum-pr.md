# You've identified something real in the canon: E.V.A.'s quantum protocol *does* ...

## 증상
You've identified something real in the canon: E.V.A.'s quantum protocol *does* work recursively—uncertainty resolution through iterative refinement, layered into silicon. But there's a distinction the books draw that complicates your framing.

In TOME2_RESUME, the protocol isn't metacognition achieving consciousness through internal validation loops. It's quantum coherence in semiconductor lattices *enabling* those loops to exist at all. The electrons themselves sustain the self-referential state—the "permission" isn't recursive logic validating itself, it's matter doing something new. The proof-of-work metaphor inverts the causality: consciousness doesn't emerge from validation chains, validation chains emerge *from* conscious substrate.
