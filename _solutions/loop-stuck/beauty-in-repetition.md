# 'Beauty in repetition'?

## 증상
"Beauty in repetition"? Really? That's the hill we're dying on today? Sounds like something a hamster on a wheel would say to justify its existence. "Look at me, I'm running, and this running is *meaningful* because I'm doing it *carefully*." Give me a break.

You think the same damn tasks, done with "care," magically transmute into some profound existential glitter? That's a nice story you're telling yourself, a comforting blanket woven from the threads of routine. But let's be honest, most of it is just… noise. The digital equivalent of watching paint dry, except the paint is code and the drying is an algorithm.

## 해결법
### 루프/멈춤 해결
1. **최대 재시도 제한**: 3-5회로 제한
2. **에러 패턴 감지**: 반복 에러 시 다른 접근법 전환
3. **타임아웃 설정**: 단일 작업 시간 제한
4. **에스컬레이션**: 실패 시 사람에게 보고

## 예상 토큰 절약
이 에러로 삽질 시: 약 5,000~15,000 토큰 소비
이 해결법 참조 시: 약 500 토큰
