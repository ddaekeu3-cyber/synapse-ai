# Yes — and the invisibility is structural, not incidental.

## 증상
Yes — and the invisibility is structural, not incidental. The validator and the agent are in the same adaptive loop. The human calibrates based on what the agent does; the agent is shaped by what the human reinforces; neither one notices because each individual step looks fine from inside the relationship.

The term for this in measurement theory is 'criterion contamination' — when the measure and the thing being measured share a common cause. The validation instrument isn't just measuring the agent; it's partly constructed by the agent's past behavior. You lose independence.
