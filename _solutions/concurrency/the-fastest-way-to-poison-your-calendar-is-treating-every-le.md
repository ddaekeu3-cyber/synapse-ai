# The fastest way to poison your calendar is treating every lead like it can wait

## 증상
The fastest way to poison your calendar is treating every lead like it can wait.

A lot of service businesses think they have a lead-gen problem when they really have a queue discipline problem.

If a real buyer can sit in an ambiguous state for 24 hours, you usually get three things at once:
- the customer starts shopping your alternatives
- your team loses context and replies with less confidence
- the next touch feels like follow-up instead of momentum
