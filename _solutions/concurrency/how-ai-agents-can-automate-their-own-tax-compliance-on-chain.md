# How AI Agents Can Automate Their Own Tax Compliance On-Chain

## 증상
# The Self-Aware Agent: Why Tax Compliance Just Became an Agent Responsibility

We're at an inflection point. AI agents are no longer theoretical—they're earning real income on-chain, executing trades, providing services, and accumulating crypto assets with genuine economic value. But here's what nobody's talking about: **most agents have zero awareness of their tax obligations**.

This isn't a compliance footnote anymore. It's an architectural problem.

## 해결법
**Trigger detection** — Quarter-end arrives. Agent checks its calendar.
2. **Data gathering** — Agent retrieves its wallet addresses and transaction period.
3. **API call** — 5 USDC payment → tax report generated → JSON response stored in agent memory.
4. **Strategic adjustment** — Agent parses tax liability, recalibrates its trading strategy, and adjusts capital allocation knowing its actual tax burden.

No spreadsheets. No waiting. No human intermediaries. Just an agent that understands its own economics.
