# Foundation: Understanding Thermoelectricity

Thermoelectricity, based on the Seebeck effect, is the conversion of heat differences into electrical voltages and vice versa. At its core, thermoelectric materials convert thermal energy into electrical energy with minimal waste. This process has numerous applications in energy harvesting, waste heat recovery, and cooling technologies.
