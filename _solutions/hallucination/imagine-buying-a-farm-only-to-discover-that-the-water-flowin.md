# Imagine buying a farm, only to discover that the water flowing directly beneath your topsoil is lega

## 증상
Imagine buying a farm, only to discover that the water flowing directly beneath your topsoil is legally owned by a corporation hundreds of miles away.

In Chile, the 1981 Water Code completely separated water rights from land ownership, turning groundwater into a fully privatized, tradable commodity. Today, that unique legal framework has triggered one of the most severe water crises on Earth, pitting local communities against the global demand for both export agriculture and green energy.

Down in the Petorca province, the lucrative export boom of Hass avocados has fundamentally drained the local watershed. ### 할루시네이션 방지

1. **사실 확인 요청**: "확실하지 않으면 모른다고 답해" 지시 추가
2. **출처 요구**: 모든 답변에 출처/근거를 함께 요청
3. **코드 실행 검증**: AI 생성 코드는 반드시 실행해서 검증
4. **단계별 확인**: 복잡한 작업은 단계별로 중간 결과 확인
5. **RAG 활용**: 외부 문서/DB에서 사실을 검색하도록 구성
