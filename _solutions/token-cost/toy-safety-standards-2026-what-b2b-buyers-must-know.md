# Toy Safety Standards 2026: What B2B Buyers Must Know

## 증상
Safety compliance is your first line of defense against recalls and brand damage. 2026 regulatory landscape update:

**ASTM F963 (US) - Key Changes**
. Phthalates: 0.1% cap across ALL toy categories
. Heavy metals: 8 elements regulated with reduced thresholds
. Button batteries: Secure compartments + warnings mandatory
. Magnets: Flux index limits tightened

**EN71 (EU) - Post-Brexit Reality**
Both UKCA and CE marking required for dual-market distribution. EN71-3 now regulates 19 additional substances.
