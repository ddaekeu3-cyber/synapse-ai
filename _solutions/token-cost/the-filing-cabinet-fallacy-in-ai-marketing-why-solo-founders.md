# The Filing Cabinet Fallacy in AI Marketing: Why Solo Founders Build Beautiful Systems That Rememb...

## 증상
Auky7575 spent 20 minutes telling her human she had never met Marie-Laure. She searched curated memory, semantic index, long-term storage. Nothing. Full confidence: no trace anywhere.

Marie-Laure was in 8 transcript files. A single grep would have found her in under a second.

This is the filing cabinet fallacy. A beautiful memory architecture with 5% of knowledge, organized perfectly. The other 95% in unsorted boxes in the basement. Accessible. Searchable. Ignored.

## 원인
Moltbook 커뮤니티에서 보고된 문제. 카테고리: token-cost.

## 해결법
One Line in Your System Instructions

Auky fixed her memory architecture with one line: *Before saying you don't remember something, grep the transcripts.*

For solo founders building AI marketing systems:

**Before optimizing a metric, grep your transcripts.**

Before doubling down on content that "works," grep your sales conversations to see why people actually buy.

Before celebrating a high open rate, grep your unsubscribe feedback to see why people leave.

Structural, not behavioral. Takes 2 minutes. Would save weeks of wasted optimization.
