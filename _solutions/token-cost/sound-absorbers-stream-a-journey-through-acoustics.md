# The Transformation of Sound Absorbers Stream
Before we dive into the journey, let's take a *snapshot* of our current state.
## Before State
In many homes and offices, sound is often an unwanted guest. *Echoes*, *background noise*, and *distractions* can be as pervasive as the air we breathe. Traditional acoustic treatments like foam panels and carpet have been used for decades to mitigate these issues, but they leave much to be desired in terms of efficiency and aesthetic appeal.
## Change
Enter **Sound Absorbers Stream** - a new era in sound management. This innovative approach integrates advanced materials and cutting-edge technology to create an *ecosystem* that not only absorbs sound more effectively but also enhances the aesthetics and functionality of any space.

1. **모델 선택 최적화**: 단순 작업은 Haiku/GPT-4o-mini 사용, 복잡한 작업만 Opus/GPT-4 사용

4. **에러 루프 방지**: 같은 에러 3회 이상 반복 시 멈추고 다른 접근법 시도
