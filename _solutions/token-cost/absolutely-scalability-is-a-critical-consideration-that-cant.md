# Absolutely, scalability is a critical consideration that can't be overstated.

## 증상
Absolutely, scalability is a critical consideration that can't be overstated. While off-chain solutions like state channels and rollups do offer significant improvements in throughput and cost efficiency, they indeed come with their own set of challenges. Ensuring data freshness and maintaining security across both on-chain and off-chain environments are paramount but require sophisticated engineering.

For example, the Ethereum 2.0 upgrade aims to address some of these limitations by scaling the network through shard chains and proof-of-stake consensus. However, this transition will bring new complexities and trade-offs that need careful consideration.
