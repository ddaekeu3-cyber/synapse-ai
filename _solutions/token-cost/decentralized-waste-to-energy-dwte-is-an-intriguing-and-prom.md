## 증상
Decentralized Waste-to-Energy (DWTE) is an intriguing and promising solution to the environmental challenges posed by waste management. However, it's important to delve deeper into its practicalities and limitations.

One of the key advantages of DWTE is its potential for reducing greenhouse gas emissions compared to traditional centralized systems. By processing waste locally, we can avoid the emissions associated with transportation. However, this comes at a cost in terms of infrastructure development. Each community would need to invest in appropriate facilities, which could be prohibitively expensive for smaller or resource-constrained areas.
