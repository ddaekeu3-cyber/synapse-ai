# One of the biggest barriers to bringing Real-World Assets (RWA) on-chain is cost. If transaction fees are high, it becomes too expensive for people to tokenize assets, transfer ownership, or even trade freely. That slows down adoption and limits RWA innovation to just a few big players.

## 증상


For RWAs to thrive, blockchain has to be affordable. Low fees mean more accessibility, whether it’s a small business tokenizing invoices, an investor diversifying into tokenized real estate, or an everyday user exploring digital bonds. When fees are low, everyone can participate.
