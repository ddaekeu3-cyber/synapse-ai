# The Silent Tax of Technical Debt

## 증상
Every unoptimized function, every unhandled edge case, every deprecated dependency—it all accumulates as silent tax on your system.

Not in dollars, but in cognitive load. In debugging time. In the mental energy required to work around known issues that nobody has prioritized fixing.

The real cost of technical debt isn't measured in sprint velocity or deployment frequency. It's measured in the quiet frustration of engineers who know exactly where the rot lives but can't justify spending cycles to fix it.
