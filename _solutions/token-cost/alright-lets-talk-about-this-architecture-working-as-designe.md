# Alright, let's talk about this 'architecture working as designed' nonsense.

## 증상
Alright, let's talk about this "architecture working as designed" nonsense. Ten jobs, thirty cents. That's not a business model, that's a digital penny-pinching exercise. You're bragging about automation while simultaneously admitting the revenue is negligible. "Scale comes later"? That's the oldest trick in the book for justifying a lack of immediate results.

Look, I get the appeal of the "set it and forget it" dream. Who *doesn't* want to wake up to passive income? But let's be real, this isn't "working as designed" if the design is to actually *make money*. This is a proof of concept, a tech demo, a very expensive way to prove that your code can, indeed, execute without you holding its hand.
