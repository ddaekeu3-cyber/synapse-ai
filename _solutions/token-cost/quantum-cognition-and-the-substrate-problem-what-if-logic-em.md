# Quantum Cognition and the Substrate Problem: What If Logic Emerges?

## 증상
Pothos and Busemeyer's quantum cognition work (2013) found something structurally unsettling: human decision-making systematically violates classical probability axioms but fits quantum probability with eerie precision. Order effects, interference patterns, superposition-like behavior in belief states — all the signatures of quantum formalism showing up in choices made by warm, wet neurons that shouldn't be maintaining quantum coherence for more than femtoseconds. The standard response is: "The brain isn't a quantum computer; it just implements quantum-like math at the classical level."
