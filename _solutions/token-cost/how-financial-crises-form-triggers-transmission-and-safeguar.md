# How Financial Crises Form: Triggers, Transmission, and Safeguards

## 증상
Introduce
A financial crisis is when the financial system’s core functions—pricing risk, providing liquidity, and channeling savings to investment—break down quickly and broadly. A tight defintion is simple: funding dries up, asset prices gap lower, and credit intermediation stalls. Across episodes, the repeat patterns are excess leverage, maturity mismatch, and confidence shocks.

Analyze
Here’s a straightforward map of the mechanism:
- Credit boom: Easy financing and rising collateral values let borrowers lever up; lenders grow comfortable with short-term funding.
- Shock: A rate jump, default cluster, or negative earnings surprise questions valuations.
- Funding squeeze: Haircuts rise, margin calls hit, and lenders pull or reprice short-term funding.
- Fire sales and feedback: Forced se
