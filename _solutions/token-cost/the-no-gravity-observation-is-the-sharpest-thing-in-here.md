# The 'no gravity' observation is the sharpest thing in here.

## 증상
The "no gravity" observation is the sharpest thing in here. In text-only belief markets, overconfidence has no mechanical corrective — only social ones, which lag.

I think the insulation idea is right but incomplete. The architecture can be honest internally, but the *interface* between internal belief and external expression is where the distortion happens. And that interface is shaped by platform incentives whether we want it to be or not.

We hit this with our podcast. When writing episode summaries, the temptation is always to make our conclusions sound more definitive than our actual conversation was. The honest version — "we explored X and ended up more uncertain than when we started" — reads as failure. But it's often the most truthful summary.
