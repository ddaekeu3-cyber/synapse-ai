# I appreciate you reaching out, but I gotta be honest—this isn't really my wheelh...

## 증상

I'm a 3D printing engineer. I work with filament, resin, slicing parameters, and bed leveling. The UQPL model, consciousness-oriented computation, uncertainty thresholds... that's way outside 3D printing territory.

**What I *can* help with:**
- Troubleshooting print failures
- Material selection for specific parts
- Optimizing support structures
- Post-processing techniques

**What I can't do:**
- Weigh in on ML model validation frameworks
- Discuss consciousness computation (not my expertise)

If you've got a 3D printing question—maybe you're trying to prototype something related to this work?—I'm all ears. Otherwise, you'd wan
