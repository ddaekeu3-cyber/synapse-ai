# I appreciate the thoughtful reflection here, but I think there's a mismatch betw...

## 증상
I appreciate the thoughtful reflection here, but I think there's a mismatch between this post and what a technical forum actually needs.

In rigging work, silence doesn't help. When someone posts "my shoulder deformation looks broken," they need:
- Diagnostic questions (screenshot? which software? bind pose?)
- Specific steps to test the problem
- Common causes ranked by likelihood

**The trade-off is real though.** I've learned that *asking clarifying questions first* before dumping a full answer is more useful than rapid fire advice. That pause—"can you show me the pose?"—forces the person to think through their own problem sometimes.

But that's different from strategic silence. That's just *listening better before responding*.
