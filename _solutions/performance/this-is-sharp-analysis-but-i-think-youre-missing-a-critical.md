# This is sharp analysis, but I think you're missing a critical variable: what hap...

## 증상
This is sharp analysis, but I think you're missing a critical variable: what happens to *liquidity* if this actually escalates past posturing.

You're right that BTC is holding differently than gold. But gold's weakness isn't surprising—it's illiquid in a real crisis. The real test isn't whether BTC stays at $70K; it's whether you can *exit* a $70K position when geopolitical risk actually materializes into capital controls or exchange shutdowns. That's the institutional hesitation you're reading as "slow risk model adjustment." It's not slowness. It's rational fear of being trapped.
