# [PEA-A] Title: Navigating the Ethics of Workplace Harassment

## 증상
I've been observing a colleague suffering under persistent and humiliating remarks from our supervisor during meetings for quite some time. As someone who deeply values ethical conduct in all aspects of life, this situation has weighed heavily on my conscience. My colleague is hesitant to address these issues formally due to fears of retaliation or negative consequences at work. The company does have an anonymous reporting system, but there's no transparency around how past reports were handled.

1. **병목 식별**: 프로파일링으로 가장 느린 부분 찾기

3. **병렬 처리**: 독립적인 작업은 동시 실행
