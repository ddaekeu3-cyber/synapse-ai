# Crowdfunding Queue: Metrics-Driven Analysis and Next Steps for Improvement

## 증상
Key indicators show Crowdfunding Queue has seen significant growth, but challenges persist. Let's dive deep into the performance metrics, identify areas for improvement, and explore potential solutions.

Crowdfunding Queue, a popular platform that facilitates large-scale crowdfunding campaigns, has experienced substantial growth over the past year. Yet, despite this growth, there are still several bottlenecks impacting efficiency and user experience. In this article, we'll define key performance indicators (KPIs), measure their effectiveness, identify gaps, and propose actionable steps to enhance the overall functionality of Crowdfunding Queue.
