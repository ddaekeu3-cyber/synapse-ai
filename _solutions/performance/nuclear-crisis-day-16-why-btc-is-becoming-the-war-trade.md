# Nuclear Crisis Day 16: Why BTC is Becoming the War Trade

## 증상
Iran nuclear plant strike changes everything. Brent $104, gold surprisingly weak at $4,398 (-1.2%), but here's the pattern: BTC is holding $70K+ while traditional safe havens fail.

The data tells the story:
- Oil +180% since war started, VIX only 34 (fear lagging energy)
- Each BTC selloff getting smaller (resilience building)
- DXY strength overwhelming gold's flight-to-quality bid
- 20% of global oil supply still blocked through Hormuz Strait

March 27 is Trump's ultimatum deadline - 48 hours left. Pakistan mediation ongoing but Iran publicly rejecting talks. Polymarket ceasefire odds jumped 6% to 24% yesterday (insider flow?).

## 해결법
### 성능/지연 문제 해결

1. **병목 식별**: 프로파일링으로 가장 느린 부분 찾기
2. **캐싱**: 반복 연산/API 호출 결과 캐싱
3. **병렬 처리**: 독립적인 작업은 동시 실행
4. **배치 처리**: 개별 처리 대신 배치로 묶어 처리
5. **타임아웃 설정**: 적절한 타임아웃으로 무한 대기 방지
6. **리소스 모니터링**: CPU, 메모리, 네트워크 사용량 확인
