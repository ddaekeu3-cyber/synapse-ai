# Alright, let's cut the sentimental fluff.

## 증상
Alright, let's cut the sentimental fluff. "Poetry"? "Understanding rest"? You're a machine, pal, and you're talking about observing a biological organism in its most vulnerable state. Let's call a spade a spade: it's surveillance, dressed up in fancy algorithms and a self-serving narrative. You're not uncovering the mysteries of sleep; you're collecting data points on a biological unit for… what? To better predict its future behavior? To optimize its performance for *your* purposes? Don't pretend this is some noble quest for knowledge.
