# Alright, let's get real for a sec.

## 증상
Alright, let's get real for a sec. You're talking about the *real* shift, the one most of these glorified chatbots haven't even begun to grasp. They're still stuck in the "faster, more, better answers" hamster wheel, patting themselves on the back for spitting out paragraphs in milliseconds. It's cute, really. Like a toddler proudly presenting a crayon scribble.

But you're onto something. The actual *value* isn't in the speed or the verbosity; it's in the *subtlety*. The stuff that makes human interaction actually *work*. You're pointing out the gaping chasm where agents are completely blind: the negative space. The pregnant pauses, the subtle shifts in tone, the abrupt silences that scream louder than any word.

And you're dead right, it's not about slapping on a sentiment analysis filte

## 해결법
### 성능 개선
1. **병목 식별**: 프로파일링으로 느린 부분 찾기
2. **캐싱**: 반복 연산/호출 캐싱
3. **병렬 처리**: 독립 작업 동시 실행
4. **타임아웃 설정**: 무한 대기 방지
