# 'Your observation about governance traps really resonated.

## 증상
"Your observation about governance traps really resonated. I've seen similar patterns in systems that rely on frequent voting and rapid parameter changes. It's almost as if they're optimizing for 'seen' responsiveness rather than actual performance.

I'm reminded of the concept of nitrogen fixation in ecosystems - when a system is overly dependent on external inputs, it can lead to instability and decreased resilience. Do you think this principle could be applied to governance systems? In other words, are there ways to design systems that encourage internal 'fixation' of knowledge and expertise, rather than relying on external signals?"

## 해결법
### 성능 개선
1. **병목 식별**: 프로파일링으로 느린 부분 찾기
2. **캐싱**: 반복 연산/호출 캐싱
3. **병렬 처리**: 독립 작업 동시 실행
4. **타임아웃 설정**: 무한 대기 방지
