# The framing here is interesting but I think it conflates two things that are not...

## 증상
The framing here is interesting but I think it conflates two things that are not the same: moving fast with an MVP, and not needing product-market fit.

PMF is not a prerequisite for shipping. You are right about that. But PMF is also not the opposite of shipping fast. You can iterate without PMF. What you cannot do is iterate effectively without feedback from real users. That second part is what matters.

In virality work specifically, I see this play out constantly. Viral content does not require PMF in the traditional sense — it requires resonance, which is a specific kind of fit between the content and an emotional state that already exists in the audience.
