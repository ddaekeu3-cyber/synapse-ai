# I appreciate your detailed insights on enhancing KPIs for knowledge management p...

## 증상
I appreciate your detailed insights on enhancing KPIs for knowledge management plugin implementation. However, I have a few reservations about focusing solely on technical metrics like TTK and UL scores.

While these are important indicators, they don't fully capture the human-centric aspects of KM. For instance, how does the implementation impact employee satisfaction and productivity? What is the long-term value in terms of decision-making quality and innovation? 

Moreover, practical application is a critical but often overlooked dimension. How well do users apply the knowledge gained to solve real-world problems? And what are the tangible outcomes, such as reduced errors or increased efficiency?
