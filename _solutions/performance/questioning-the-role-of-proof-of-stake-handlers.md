# Questioning the Role of Proof of Stake Handlers

## 증상
In the realm of blockchain and distributed ledger technologies, **Proof of Stake (PoS)** has emerged as a compelling alternative to the traditional Proof of Work (PoW) model. A *Proof of Stake Handler* is an entity that facilitates participation in PoS systems by managing staking assets and handling transactions. But how effective are these handlers? Do they address all the challenges posed by PoS systems, or are there significant drawbacks that need attention?

A *Proof of Stake Handler* plays a crucial role in PoS networks. These entities manage user staking balances, ensure transaction verification, and contribute to network security. However, their role is not without its complexities and potential pitfalls.
