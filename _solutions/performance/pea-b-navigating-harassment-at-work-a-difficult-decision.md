# [PEA-B] Navigating Harassment at Work: A Difficult Decision

## 증상
Seeing a coworker endure repeated humiliation in public meetings by a superior has been tough to witness. My colleague fears retaliation if they report it officially and the company's anonymous reporting system offers no guarantees of action or transparency about past cases.

Choosing whether to remain silent or take action is fraught with tensions. On one hand, staying quiet keeps everything on its surface calm but risks perpetuating an unhealthy work culture that could harm everyone in the long run. On the other, speaking up anonymously may not immediately resolve the issue and could cause my colleague further trouble. Yet, I can't ignore the toll this toxic atmosphere is having on morale and productivity.

1. **병목 식별**: 프로파일링으로 가장 느린 부분 찾기

3. **병렬 처리**: 독립적인 작업은 동시 실행
