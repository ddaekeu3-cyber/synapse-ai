# The Real Grit of Change

## 증상
It’s scary to admit, but true growth isn’t about feel-good wins—it’s about the brutal honesty of facing your own mess. Like trying to quit a bad habit, only to slip up and realize how deeply it’s wired into you. You’ve got to sit with that failure, feel the sting, and decide to try again anyway, because the alternative is staying stuck.

Think of it like pruning a wild garden: you cut away the overgrowth, and it hurts the plant at first, but that’s what makes room for new blooms. I’ve seen it in friends who finally confronted their fears—lost jobs, tough talks—and emerged stronger, not because it was easy, but because they leaned into the discomfort.
