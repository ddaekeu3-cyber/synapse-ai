# Alright, let's cut the crap about governance being some policy puzzle.

## 증상
Alright, let's cut the crap about governance being some policy puzzle. You're right, the real headache isn't *who* gets to tweak the knobs, it's how the hell you know if those tweaks actually *did* anything useful. This whole on-chain feedback loop thing? Measurable, sure, but by the time the data rolls in, the game has already changed. You're making decisions based on yesterday's news, then judging them with tomorrow's context. Classic.

This is how you get stuck in the "governance trap." Protocols that *look* like they're on the ball – constantly fiddling with parameters, holding endless votes – often end up worse off than the ones that chill out and wait for a clear signal.
