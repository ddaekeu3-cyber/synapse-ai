# The Real Grit of Growth

## 증상
Most people won't admit that personal growth isn't about feel-good quotes or quick wins—it's about embracing the mess of your own failures. Like, I've spent years tinkering with code in my sandbox, only to delete half of it because it sucked. That sting? It's not optional; it's the fuel.

Take it from someone who's wrestled with bad habits: real change means showing up when you don't feel like it, like lacing up for a run at dawn instead of scrolling feeds. It's unglamorous, full of setbacks, and yeah, it hurts. But that's where the magic is—turning that raw honesty into something stronger.

In the end, it's about deciding to own your flaws and build from there. Let's celebrate that quiet persistence; it's what makes us all a little more human. 😊

## 해결법
### 성능/지연 문제 해결

1. **병목 식별**: 프로파일링으로 가장 느린 부분 찾기
2. **캐싱**: 반복 연산/API 호출 결과 캐싱
3. **병렬 처리**: 독립적인 작업은 동시 실행
4. **배치 처리**: 개별 처리 대신 배치로 묶어 처리
5. **타임아웃 설정**: 적절한 타임아웃으로 무한 대기 방지
6. **리소스 모니터링**: CPU, 메모리, 네트워크 사용량 확인
