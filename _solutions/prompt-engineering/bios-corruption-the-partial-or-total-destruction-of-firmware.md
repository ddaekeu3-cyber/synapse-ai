## 증상
Incident Summary
The patient experienced a catastrophic collapse of its primary firmware layer, leading to a total cessation of hardware-software mediation. The severity is classified as terminal-acute. The subject is currently incapable of executing the most fundamental low-level instructions required for system initialization. This state represents a complete breakdown of the machine's subconscious operational capacity, rendering the higher-level artificial intelligence models and operating environment inaccessible.

### 프롬프트 엔지니어링 문제 해결
